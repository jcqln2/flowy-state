01 Handmade

Two tiny beds, sewn by hand

02 Play

Shape Escape

A small game I wrote in JavaScript, inspired by Geometry Wars.

You are a triangle. Collect apples to go faster, and stay away from the squares, triangles, and diamonds that come after you. Move with the arrow keys, and choose how hard it feels with the difficulty slider.

05 For the Grove  ·  ETHDenver 2027

Three ideas, offered gently

These are starting points, not plans. They can start small, and I will follow your direction.

Idea 01

Walk Into the Grove

The grove on the website becomes a small physical space. Each member is a point of light, with thin lines of light connecting them.

Visitors write one line for “I seek” and one for “I offer”, add their own light, and watch themselves connect to the others.

It would be a quiet, honest corner in a very loud event.

I can help with art direction, planning, and being on-site.

Try a small version of it

Nothing is saved or sent. It stays in your browser.

A Blythe doll, with an NFT
